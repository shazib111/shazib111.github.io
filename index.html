About

I spent two years as an engineer at Fiber@Home Limited, watching over nationwide telecom and IP infrastructure — resolving 100+ weekly tickets, isolating faults across DWDM, MPLS, and GPON, and keeping distributed systems reliable under real pressure. That work taught me something most robotics engineers never have to learn early: systems fail at the connections, not just the components.

Now I'm applying that instinct to autonomous systems — most recently building a vision-guided dual-robot sorting system where a Braccio arm and a DJI RoboMaster had to perceive, decide, and hand off tasks to each other in real time. It's the same problem I used to monitor from a NOC dashboard, just embodied in hardware.

I'm currently completing an M.Sc. in AI Engineering of Autonomous Systems at THI, and transitioning this winter to Robotics and Autonomous Systems at the University of Lübeck — trading a software-first curriculum for one grounded in mechanics, electrical engineering, and medical/service robotics research.
